# Meals By Simone

Build a landing page for "Meals By Simone," a meal-prep and nutrition membership site run by a certified nutritionist and strength & conditioning coach. The tone is warm, encouraging, and family-focused — helping busy women stop stressing over "what's for dinner." VISUAL STYLE: Editorial and warm, not corporate. Large, high-quality lifestyle photography as the visual anchor of each section. Pair a soft script/handwritten accent font for emotive section headers with a clean, modern sans-serif for body copy. Generous whitespace between sections. Rounded, soft UI elements (buttons, cards) rather than sharp corners. Warm neutral color palette (creams, soft greens or terracotta accents) — avoid a cold, tech-startup look. SECTIONS (in order): 1. Header/nav: logo left, "Login" and "Join Now" buttons right 2. Hero: bold headline about never asking "what's for dinner" again, subheading, "Start Free Trial" CTA button, and a "Watch Video" link. Large lifestyle image or video thumbnail alongside. 3. Testimonials strip: 8-10 short rotating/scrolling customer quotes 4. Video section: embedded intro video with a CTA button below it 5. "How it works" — 3-4 numbered steps (e.g. Sign Up, Grab Your Menu, Get Cooking, Enjoy) shown with a supporting image, not just icons 6. Founder bio: photo of Simone, her credentials and story (certified nutritionist, strength & conditioning coach, 20+ years experience, who she's worked with — professionals, athletes, families), ending with a signature-style sign-off 7. Social proof: Instagram feed embed or placeholder grid 8. Recipe gallery ("sneak peek"): grid of 6 recipe photos with names underneath, in the fraiche-style card layout 9. Benefits grid: 3 columns — Save Time, Save Money, Stay Healthy — each with a simple icon/image and one line of supporting copy 10. Pricing: 2 plan cards (monthly vs. annual with a "Best Value" badge and savings callout), each with a "Sign Up Now" button 11. Newsletter signup block 12. Footer: social links (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube), nav links, copyright Make it fully responsive and mobile-first. Use placeholder images for now — I'll swap in real photography afterward.
